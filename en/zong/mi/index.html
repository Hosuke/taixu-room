Esoteric (Zhenyan)

唐密開元三大士——胎藏金剛、三密相應

Doctrinal map

Dharmadhātu–perfect-awakening system 大乘不共法 · 法界圓覺系

Grouped by Taixu’s three Mahāyāna systems: Emptiness and Wisdom (Sanlun); Consciousness-Only (Weishi, and the Nanshan Vinaya, which Taixu placed here); Perfect Awakening (Tiantai, Huayan, Chan, Pure Land, Esoteric). Open a school to see its texts and masters.

Primary texts

  • Mahāvairocana Sutra 大日經 — An esoteric scripture on awakening, mantra, and maṇḍala practice, central to Tang Esoteric Buddhism and Shingon.

  • Explanation of the Meaning of the Mahāvairocana Sūtra 大日經義釋 — This recension of the Mahāvairocana commentary explains the sūtra's teachings and ritual system in fourteen fascicles.

Patriarchs

  • Shanwuwei (Śubhakarasiṃha) 637-735 — Śubhakarasiṃha (637–735) was an Indian monk who arrived in Chang'an in 716.
  • Jingangzhi (Vajrabodhi) 671-741 — Vajrabodhi (671–741) was an Indian monk who travelled by sea through Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia before reaching China in 720.
  • Yixing 683-727 — Yixing (683–727), born Zhang Sui, was a Tang monk, astronomer and mathematician.
  • Bukong (Amoghavajra) 705-774 — Born in 705, Amoghavajra became Vajrabodhi's disciple while young and accompanied him to Tang China.
  • Huiguo 746-805 — Huiguo (746–805) was a disciple of Amoghavajra and an esoteric Buddhist teacher at Qinglong Monastery in Chang'an.
  • Kūkai 774-835 — Kūkai (774–835), born in Sanuki, travelled to Tang China in 804.
  • Bukesiyi 八世紀 — Bukesiyi was a monk of Lingmiao Monastery in Silla, active in the eighth century.
  • Dānapāla 十至十一世紀 — Dānapāla, known in Chinese as Shihu, was an Indian monk active in the Northern Song translation institute.
  • Feixi 八世紀 — Feixi was a Tang monk associated with Dasheng Qianfu Monastery.
