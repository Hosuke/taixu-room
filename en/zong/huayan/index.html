Huayan

法藏五教——事事無礙、十玄門、六相圓融

Doctrinal map

Dharmadhātu–perfect-awakening system 大乘不共法 · 法界圓覺系

Grouped by Taixu’s three Mahāyāna systems: Emptiness and Wisdom (Sanlun); Consciousness-Only (Weishi, and the Nanshan Vinaya, which Taixu placed here); Perfect Awakening (Tiantai, Huayan, Chan, Pure Land, Esoteric). Open a school to see its texts and masters.

Primary texts

  • Flower Ornament Sutra 大方廣佛華嚴經 — The eighty-fascicle Chinese Avataṃsaka collection, important to Huayan Buddhism.
  • Flower Ornament Sutra (Sixty-Fascicle Version) 大方廣佛華嚴經（六十卷本） — The earlier sixty-fascicle Chinese Avataṃsaka collection.
  • Flower Ornament Sutra: Entry into the Inconceivable Realm of Liberation and Samantabhadra's Practices and Vows 大方廣佛華嚴經入不思議解脫境界普賢行願品 — The forty-fascicle version of the Gaṇḍavyūha, concluding with Samantabhadra's vows.
  • Sutra of Perfect Enlightenment 大方廣圓覺修多羅了義經 — A Chinese Buddhist scripture on awakening, meditation, and the purification of delusion.
  • Awakening of Faith in the Mahāyāna 大乘起信論 — A treatise on mind and awakening traditionally attributed to Aśvaghoṣa; its origin is debated.
  • Doctrinal Distinctions of the Huayan One Vehicle 華嚴一乘教義分齊章 — Fazang's classification of teachings and exposition of the Huayan one vehicle.
  • Treatise on the Golden Lion 華嚴金師子章 — Fazang's explanation of Huayan interdependence through the image of a golden lion.
  • Calming and Contemplation in the Five Huayan Teachings 華嚴五教止觀 — The work organizes contemplation into five teachings, moving from elementary analysis toward the interpenetration of all phenomena.
  • Guide to Exploring the Profound Meaning of the Avataṃsaka Sūtra 大方廣佛華嚴經搜玄分齊通智方軌 — Zhiyan comments on the sixty-fascicle Avataṃsaka Sūtra, developing accounts of dependent arising and the interrelation of its teachings.
  • Ten Mysterious Gates of the One Vehicle of Huayan 華嚴一乘十玄門 — The work explains ten ways in which phenomena interpenetrate within the Huayan understanding of the one vehicle.
  • Record of Exploring the Profound Meaning of the Avataṃsaka Sūtra 華嚴經探玄記 — Fazang comments on the sixty-fascicle Avataṃsaka Sūtra and elaborates Huayan doctrine through its sequence of assemblies and teachings.
  • Contemplation of Ending Delusion and Returning to the Source 修華嚴奧旨妄盡還源觀 — The work presents Huayan contemplation through the relation of the source to its functions and the integration of insight with compassionate conduct.
  • Commentary on the Awakening of Faith in the Mahāyāna 大乘起信論義記 — Fazang explains the Awakening of Faith through the nature of mind, awakening and delusion, and the development of faith and practice.
  • Commentary on the Avataṃsaka Sūtra 大方廣佛華嚴經疏 — Chengguan provides a detailed commentary on the eighty-fascicle Avataṃsaka Sūtra, explaining its chapters and Huayan accounts of the realm of reality.
  • Profound Mirror of the Huayan Realm of Reality 華嚴法界玄鏡 — Chengguan comments on the contemplation of the realm of reality associated with Dushun, explaining emptiness and the unobstructed relation of principle and phen…
  • Commentary on the Practices and Vows Chapter of the Avataṃsaka Sūtra 華嚴經行願品疏 — Chengguan explains the practices and vows of Samantabhadra and their place in the bodhisattva path.
  • Annotated Contemplation of the Huayan Realm of Reality 註華嚴法界觀門 — Zongmi annotates the contemplation text associated with Dushun, explaining its progression from emptiness to the mutual inclusion of phenomena.
  • Inquiry into the Origin of Humanity 原人論 — Zongmi compares Confucian, Daoist, and Buddhist accounts of human origins and orders Buddhist teachings toward an account of the awakened mind.
  • General Preface to the Collected Explanations of the Source of Chan 禪源諸詮集都序 — Zongmi compares Chan approaches and Buddhist doctrinal teachings, explaining their relation through awakening and cultivation.

Patriarchs

  • Dushun 557-640 — Dushun is regarded in Huayan tradition as its first patriarch.
  • Zhiyan 602-668 — Zhiyan was a Tang Buddhist scholar regarded as the second Huayan patriarch.
  • Fazang 643-712 — Fazang (643–712) was born in Chang'an to a family of Sogdian ancestry and studied under Zhiyan.
  • Chengguan 738-839 — Chengguan wrote a major commentary and subcommentary on the eighty-fascicle Avataṃsaka Sūtra.
  • Zongmi 780-841 — Zongmi combined the Huayan scholarship of Chengguan with Chan training received from Suizhou Daoyuan in the Heze lineage.
  • Hanshan Deqing 1546-1623 — Hanshan Deqing wrote commentaries on the Diamond, Śūraṅgama, Laṅkāvatāra and other sūtras while advocating both Chan and Pure Land practice.
