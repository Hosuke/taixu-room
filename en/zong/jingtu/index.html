Pure Land

持名念佛——彌陀本願、帶業往生、三根普被

Doctrinal map

Dharmadhātu–perfect-awakening system 大乘不共法 · 法界圓覺系

Grouped by Taixu’s three Mahāyāna systems: Emptiness and Wisdom (Sanlun); Consciousness-Only (Weishi, and the Nanshan Vinaya, which Taixu placed here); Perfect Awakening (Tiantai, Huayan, Chan, Pure Land, Esoteric). Open a school to see its texts and masters.

Primary texts

  • Larger Sutra of Immeasurable Life 無量壽經 — A Pure Land scripture on Amitābha's vows and the land of Sukhāvatī.
  • Contemplation Sutra 觀無量壽經 — A Pure Land scripture describing contemplations of Amitābha and rebirth in Sukhāvatī.
  • Amitābha Sutra 阿彌陀經 — The shorter Pure Land scripture on Sukhāvatī and recollection of Amitābha.
  • Commentary on the Contemplation Sutra 觀無量壽佛經疏 — Shandao's Pure Land commentary on the Contemplation Sutra.
  • Commentary and Subcommentary on the Amitābha Sutra 阿彌陀經疏鈔 — Yunqi Zhuhong's Pure Land commentary on the Amitābha Sutra.
  • Essential Explanation of the Amitābha Sutra 阿彌陀經要解 — Ouyi Zhixu's interpretation of the Amitābha Sutra and Pure Land practice.
  • Medicine Buddha Sutra 藥師琉璃光如來本願功德經 — A scripture on Bhaiṣajyaguru's vows and associated devotional practices.
  • Sutra on Contemplating Maitreya's Ascent to Tuṣita Heaven 觀彌勒菩薩上生兜率天經 — Contemplation of Maitreya and rebirth in Tuṣita Heaven.
  • Sutra of Maitreya's Descent and Buddhahood 佛說彌勒下生成佛經 — A scripture describing the future buddhahood and teaching of Maitreya.
  • Treatise on the Sūtra of Immeasurable Life 無量壽經優波提舍 — Vasubandhu describes aspiration for birth in Amitābha's land through verses and an explanation of five gates of contemplative practice.
  • Commentary on the Verses of Aspiration for Birth 無量壽經優婆提舍願生偈註 — Tanluan comments on Vasubandhu's Pure Land treatise, explaining the five gates of practice and the role of Amitābha's vows.
  • Collection on Peace and Bliss 安樂集 — Daochuo distinguishes the path of sages from the Pure Land path and explains aspiration and practices for birth in Amitābha's land.
  • Verses Praising Birth in the Pure Land 往生禮讚偈 — Shandao arranges verses, invocations, and acts of worship for devotion to Amitābha and aspiration for birth in the Pure Land.
  • Method of Contemplating Amitābha's Ocean of Marks and the Merits of Samādhi 觀念阿彌陀佛相海三昧功德法門 — Shandao explains the contemplation and recollection of Amitābha, supported by scriptural passages on practice and its benefits.
  • Treatise on the Jewel-King Samādhi of Buddha Recollection 念佛三昧寶王論 — Feixi discusses buddha recollection and samādhi, relating devotional practice to contemplative insight and the bodhisattva path.
  • Commentary on the Sublime Teaching of the Contemplation Sūtra 觀無量壽佛經疏妙宗鈔 — Zhili explains a commentary on the Contemplation Sūtra through Tiantai accounts of mind and the contemplation of the Buddha.
  • Treatise on Birth and Nonbirth in the Pure Land 淨土生無生論 — Chuandeng explains how aspiration for birth in the Pure Land accords with the nonarising of phenomena in Tiantai thought.
  • Ten Essentials of the Pure Land 淨土十要 — The anthology brings together Pure Land writings selected by Zhixu in a version abridged and annotated by Chengshi.
  • Record of Chan Master Chewu 徹悟禪師語錄 — Chewu's recorded instructions relate the recollection of Amitābha to faith, aspiration, and sustained Pure Land practice.

Patriarchs

  • Lushan Huiyuan 334-416 — Huiyuan studied under Dao'an before establishing a monastic community at Donglin Monastery on Mount Lu.
  • Tanluan 476-542 — Tanluan wrote a commentary on the Treatise on Rebirth attributed to Vasubandhu.
  • Daochuo 562-645 — Daochuo taught at Xuanzhong Monastery, where Tanluan's legacy influenced his turn to Pure Land practice.
  • Shandao 613-681 — Shandao (613–681) studied Pure Land practice under Daochuo at Xuanzhong Monastery before teaching in Chang'an.
  • Yongming Yanshou 904-975 — Yongming Yanshou studied under Tiantai Deshao and became abbot of Yongming Monastery in Hangzhou.
  • Siming Zhili 960-1028 — Siming Zhili was a Northern Song Tiantai teacher based at Yanqing Monastery in Siming.
  • Lingzhi Yuanzhao 1048-1116 — Lingzhi Yuanzhao was a Northern Song scholar of the Nanshan Vinaya tradition.
  • Lianchi Zhuhong 1535-1615 — Lianchi Zhuhong rebuilt Yunqi Monastery near Hangzhou and promoted monastic discipline and the recitation of Amitābha's name.
  • Youxi Chuandeng 1554-1628 — Youxi Chuandeng was a Ming Tiantai teacher based at Gaoming Monastery in Youxi.
  • Ouyi Zhixu 1599-1655 — Ouyi Zhixu wrote on Tiantai doctrine, Pure Land practice and monastic discipline during the late Ming and early Qing.
  • Chewu Jixing 1741-1811 — Chewu Jixing, also known as Mengdong, was a Qing Buddhist teacher trained in Chan and scriptural study.
  • Bodhiruci (Northern Wei) 六世紀 — Bodhiruci was an Indian translator who arrived in Luoyang in 508 during the Northern Wei dynasty.
  • Feixi 八世紀 — Feixi was a Tang monk associated with Dasheng Qianfu Monastery.
