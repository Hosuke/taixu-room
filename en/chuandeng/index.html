Transmission of the Lamp

Trace Chan transmission through the Jingde Record of the Transmission of the Lamp and the Compendium of the Five Lamps, from the seven Buddhas to later Chinese lineages. Of 2867 people, 961 have biographies in the Jingde record and 1212 come from the later compendium; the others appear in lists without recorded dialogues.
