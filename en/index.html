Three tiers of doctrine

Taixu’s classification: a base shared by five vehicles, a middle shared by three, and a summit of three Mahāyāna systems — all equal, all ultimate.

Eight schools, equal

Grouped by Taixu’s three Mahāyāna systems: Emptiness and Wisdom (Sanlun); Consciousness-Only (Weishi, and the Nanshan Vinaya, which Taixu placed here); Perfect Awakening (Tiantai, Huayan, Chan, Pure Land, Esoteric). Open a school to see its texts and masters.

  • Sanlun (Madhyamaka) (23 texts and 7 masters in the library) Masters：Jiumoluoshi (Kumārajīva)、Sengzhao、Bhāvaviveka (Qingbian)
  • Weishi (Yogācāra) (29 texts and 10 masters in the library) Masters：Dignāga (Chenna)、Dharmapāla (Hufa)、Xuanzang
  • Vinaya (18 texts and 6 masters in the library) Masters：Daoxuan、Yijing、Jianzhen
  • Tiantai (21 texts and 10 masters in the library) Masters：Huisi、Zhiyi、Guanding
  • Huayan (19 texts and 6 masters in the library) Masters：Dushun、Zhiyan、Fazang
  • Chan (23 texts and 79 masters in the library) Masters：Huike、Daoxin、Hongren
  • Pure Land (19 texts and 13 masters in the library) Masters：Lushan Huiyuan、Tanluan、Daochuo
  • Esoteric (Zhenyan) (259 texts and 9 masters in the library) Masters：Shanwuwei (Śubhakarasiṃha)、Jingangzhi (Vajrabodhi)、Yixing

Classics

  • Diamond Sutra 金剛般若波羅蜜經 — A Prajñāpāramitā scripture on wisdom, nonattachment, and the bodhisattva path.
  • Heart Sutra 般若波羅蜜多心經 — A short Prajñāpāramitā scripture on the emptiness of the aggregates and other Buddhist categories.
  • Middle Treatise 中論 — Nāgārjuna's verses on emptiness and dependent arising, with commentary attributed to Qingmu.
  • Hundred Treatise 百論 — A Madhyamaka treatise attributed to Āryadeva with commentary attributed to Vasu.
  • Treatise on the Twelve Gates 十二門論 — A Madhyamaka work attributed to Nāgārjuna examining emptiness through twelve lines of argument.
  • Sutra Explaining the Profound Meaning 解深密經 — A Yogācāra scripture on the three natures, consciousness, and interpretation of Buddhist teaching.
  • Treatise on the Stages of Yogic Practice 瑜伽師地論 — An extensive Yogācāra compilation on meditation, doctrine, and the bodhisattva path.
  • Demonstration of Consciousness Only 成唯識論 — A Yogācāra synthesis drawing on Indian commentaries to Vasubandhu's Thirty Verses.
  • Summary of the Great Vehicle 攝大乘論 — Asaṅga's systematic exposition of Mahayana doctrine from a Yogācāra perspective.
  • Distinguishing Phenomena and Their Nature 辨法法性論 — A Yogācāra treatise traditionally attributed to Maitreya on phenomena and their ultimate nature.
  • Lotus Sutra 妙法蓮華經 — A Mahayana scripture on the one vehicle, skillful means, and the possibility of buddhahood.
  • Flower Ornament Sutra 大方廣佛華嚴經 — The eighty-fascicle Chinese Avataṃsaka collection, important to Huayan Buddhism.
  • Awakening of Faith in the Mahāyāna 大乘起信論 — A treatise on mind and awakening traditionally attributed to Aśvaghoṣa; its origin is debated.
  • Mahāparinirvāṇa Sutra 大般涅槃經 — A Mahayana scripture on the Buddha's final nirvāṇa, buddha-nature, and liberation.
  • Śūraṅgama Sutra 楞嚴經 — A Chinese Buddhist scripture on mind, perception, meditative practice, and discipline.
