Fenyang Shanzhao

Fenyang Shanzhao was a Chan teacher in the Linji tradition and a successor of Shoushan Shengnian. His verses on earlier Chan cases contributed to the development of Song Buddhist literary practice.

汾陽善昭 · 北宋 · 946-1023

Also known as: 汾陽、汾陽昭、無德禪師、太子昭、汾州、西河師子
