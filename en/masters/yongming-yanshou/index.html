Yongming Yanshou

Yongming Yanshou studied under Tiantai Deshao and became abbot of Yongming Monastery in Hangzhou. His hundred-fascicle Record of the Source Mirror draws together scriptural quotations and doctrinal discussion. Later Chan and Pure Land traditions both claimed his legacy.

永明延壽 · 五代宋初 · 904-975
