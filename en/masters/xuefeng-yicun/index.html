Xuefeng Yicun

Xuefeng Yicun was a Chan teacher active at Mount Xuefeng near Fuzhou. Traditional genealogies place him in Deshan Xuanjian's lineage and trace the Yunmen and Fayan traditions through his community.

雪峯義存 · 唐 · 822-908
