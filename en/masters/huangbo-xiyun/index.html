Huangbo Xiyun

Huangbo Xiyun belonged to Baizhang Huaihai's lineage and taught Linji Yixuan according to Chan transmission records. The official Pei Xiu collected teachings attributed to him in the Essentials of Mind Transmission and the Wanling Record.

黃檗希運 · 唐 · ?-850

Also known as: 黃檗
