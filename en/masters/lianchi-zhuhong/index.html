Lianchi Zhuhong

Lianchi Zhuhong rebuilt Yunqi Monastery near Hangzhou and promoted monastic discipline and the recitation of Amitābha's name. His commentary on the Amitābha Sūtra and his rules and exhortations addressed both monastic and lay practice.

蓮池祩宏 · 明 · 1535-1615

Also known as: 蓮池大師
