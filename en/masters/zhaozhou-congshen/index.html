Zhaozhou Congshen

Zhaozhou Congshen was a Chan teacher in Nanquan Puyuan's lineage, later based at Guanyin Monastery in Zhaozhou. Dialogues attributed to him became widely used cases in later Chan literature.

趙州從諗 · 唐 · 778-897

Also known as: 真際從諗、真際大師、趙州真際、趙州和尚、趙州古佛、趙州諗
