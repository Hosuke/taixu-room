Jianyue Duti

Jianyue Duti was a Vinaya teacher active during the transition from the Ming to the Qing dynasty. He succeeded Sanmei Jiguang at Mount Baohua and became a major figure in its ordination tradition. His writings organize rules for restraint, procedures for communal acts, and the conduct of ordination ceremonies.

見月讀體 · 明清 · 1601-1679

Also known as: 見月、紹如、見月律師
