Youxi Chuandeng

Youxi Chuandeng was a Ming Tiantai teacher based at Gaoming Monastery in Youxi. He studied with Baisong Zhenjue and wrote on Tiantai doctrine and contemplation. His Treatise on Birth and Nonbirth in the Pure Land explains Pure Land practice through the Tiantai threefold contemplation.

幽溪傳燈 · 明 · 1554-1628
