Yang Wenhui (Yang Renshan)

Yang Wenhui founded the Jinling Buddhist Press in Nanjing in 1866 to recover and circulate Buddhist texts. He also established the Jetavana Hermitage school, attended by Taixu and other figures in the modern Buddhist revival.

楊文會 · 清末民初 · 1837-1911

Also known as: 楊仁山
