Fotuonanti (Buddhanandi)

Traditional Chan genealogies count Fotuonanti as Indian patriarch number 8, following Posumi. They name Futuomiduo as his successor; these lineage accounts do not provide an independently established biography.

佛陀難提尊者 · 印度

School affiliation：Chan、西天二十八祖

Teachers

Disciples
