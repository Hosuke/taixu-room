Shenxiu

Shenxiu trained in Hongren's community and later taught at Yuquan Monastery. Invited to the imperial court under Empress Wu, he became a prominent Chan teacher. His lineage was later labelled Northern Chan by its critics.

大通神秀 · 唐 · 606?-706

Also known as: 大通禪師
