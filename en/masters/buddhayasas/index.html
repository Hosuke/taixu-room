Buddhayaśas

Buddhayaśas was a monk from Kashmir who worked in Chang'an during the Later Qin dynasty. He collaborated with Chinese translators, including Zhu Fonian. He is associated with the Chinese translations of the Dharmaguptaka Vinaya, its precept texts, and the Long Agama.

佛陀耶舍 · 後秦 · 四至五世紀

Also known as: 佛馱耶舍、覺明、覺稱
