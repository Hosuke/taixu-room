Huisi

Huisi taught meditation on the Lotus Sūtra and trained Zhiyi, whose work shaped the Tiantai tradition. He later settled at Mount Nanyue; his writings include the Meaning of the Lotus Sūtra's Practice of Ease and Bliss.

南嶽慧思 · 南北朝 · 515-577

Also known as: 南嶽大師
