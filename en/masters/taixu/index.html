Taixu

Taixu advocated reforms of Buddhist institutions, monastic education and religious practice in modern China. He founded the Wuchang Buddhist Institute in 1922 and promoted Buddhism for human life. His teaching classifications sought to accommodate the eight Chinese Buddhist schools within a common framework.

太虛大師 · 近代 · 1890-1947

Also known as: 呂淦森、唯心
