Bukesiyi

Bukesiyi was a monk of Lingmiao Monastery in Silla, active in the eighth century. Traditional accounts identify him as a disciple of Subhakarasimha. His commentary explains the offering procedures associated with the Mahavairocana Sutra; little else is securely known about his life.

不可思議 · 唐・新羅 · 八世紀
