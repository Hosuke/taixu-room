Tanluan

Tanluan wrote a commentary on the Treatise on Rebirth attributed to Vasubandhu. It explains Pure Land practice through Amitābha's vows and the dedication of merit. Later teachers, including Daochuo and Shandao, developed themes found in this work.

曇鸞大師 · 北魏 · 476-542
