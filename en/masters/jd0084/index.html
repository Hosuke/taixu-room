Yongjia Xuanjue

Yongjia Xuanjue (665–713) was a Tang Buddhist monk associated with Yongjia. Chan transmission literature places him among Huineng's disciples, while his writings also engage with Tiantai contemplation. The Yongjia Collection presents instructions on meditation and the relation between understanding and practice.
