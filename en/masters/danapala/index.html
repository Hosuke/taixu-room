Dānapāla

Dānapāla, known in Chinese as Shihu, was an Indian monk active in the Northern Song translation institute. He arrived in the Song capital in 980 and translated a large body of Buddhist literature. His works include the extensive Chinese translation of the Sarvatathagatatattvasamgraha, an important scripture of esoteric Buddhism.

施護 · 北宋 · 十至十一世紀

Also known as: 傳法大師、明悟
