Falang

Falang was a sixth-century Chinese teacher of the Three Treatises tradition associated with Xinghuang Monastery. He received the teaching through Sengquan and transmitted it to Jizang. His place in the Sanlun lineage is preserved through accounts of his teaching and disciples rather than an extant work in the Chinese Buddhist canon.

興皇法朗 · 梁陳 · 507-581

Also known as: 興皇、興皇大師
