Zhiyan

Zhiyan was a Tang Buddhist scholar regarded as the second Huayan patriarch. Traditional accounts connect him with Dushun and identify him as a teacher of Fazang.

雲華智儼 · 唐 · 602-668

Also known as: 雲華尊者
