Siming Zhili

Siming Zhili was a Northern Song Tiantai teacher based at Yanqing Monastery in Siming. He studied under Yitong and became a leading exponent of the Shanjia interpretation of Tiantai doctrine. His commentaries on the Ten Gates of Nonduality and the Contemplation Sutra connect doctrinal analysis with contemplative practice.

四明知禮 · 北宋 · 960-1028

Also known as: 四明尊者、法智大師、四明法智
