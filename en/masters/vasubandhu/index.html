Shiqin (Vasubandhu)

Vasubandhu wrote the Abhidharmakośa and its commentary, examining the categories and arguments of Buddhist scholasticism. Works associated with his Yogācāra teaching include the Twenty Verses and Thirty Verses on consciousness, both extensively studied in East Asia.

世親菩薩 · 印度 · c.4-5世紀

Also known as: 天親
