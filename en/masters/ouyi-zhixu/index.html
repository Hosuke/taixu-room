Ouyi Zhixu

Ouyi Zhixu wrote on Tiantai doctrine, Pure Land practice and monastic discipline during the late Ming and early Qing. His works include Essential Explanation of the Amitābha Sūtra and a critical catalogue of the Buddhist canon.

蕅益智旭 · 明末清初 · 1599-1655

Also known as: 蕅益
