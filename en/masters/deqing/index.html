Hanshan Deqing

Hanshan Deqing wrote commentaries on the Diamond, Śūraṅgama, Laṅkāvatāra and other sūtras while advocating both Chan and Pure Land practice. His collected writings, the Dream-Roaming Collection, preserve sermons, letters and autobiographical material from his career in late Ming China.

憨山德清 · 明 · 1546-1623

Also known as: 憨山、憨山大師
