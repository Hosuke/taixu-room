Yunmen Wenyan

Yunmen Wenyan trained under Xuefeng Yicun and later taught at Mount Yunmen in Guangdong. His recorded sayings became important sources for Chan case collections. The Yunmen tradition takes its name from his teaching centre.

雲門文偃 · 唐末五代 · 864-949

Also known as: 雲門
