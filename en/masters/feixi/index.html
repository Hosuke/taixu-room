Feixi

Feixi was a Tang monk associated with Dasheng Qianfu Monastery. He participated in Amoghavajra's translation activities and studied both esoteric practice and Pure Land devotion. His Treatise on the Jewel-King Samadhi of Buddha Recollection discusses the doctrinal basis and practice of recollecting the Buddha.

飛錫 · 唐 · 八世紀

Also known as: 釋飛錫
