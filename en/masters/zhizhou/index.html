Zhizhou

Zhizhou studied under Huizhao and continued the Faxiang teaching associated with Kuiji. His Explication of the Subtleties explains difficult passages in the commentarial tradition of the Treatise Establishing Consciousness Only. Later accounts recognize him as the third patriarch of the Ci'en lineage.

濮陽智周 · 唐 · 678-733

Also known as: 濮陽、濮陽大師
