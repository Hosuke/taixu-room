Lingzhi Yuanzhao

Lingzhi Yuanzhao was a Northern Song scholar of the Nanshan Vinaya tradition. He served at several monasteries, including Lingzhi Chongfu, and also advocated Pure Land practice. His Zichi ji, Xingzong ji, and Jiyuan ji explain Daoxuan's major works on monastic discipline.

靈芝元照 · 北宋 · 1048-1116

Also known as: 靈芝尊者、安忍子、大智律師、靈芝照
